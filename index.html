PunyWhy
The same domain can be two different ASCII names, depending on the rules.
Open the app
faß.de is xn--fa-hia.de in current browsers and fass.de under the old IDNA2003 rules. Different names, different owners.

What it does

  • Converts a domain to its ASCII (xn--) form under UTS 46, IDNA2003 and IDNA2008.
  • Decodes xn-- names back to Unicode.
  • Shows what your own browser does with the same name.
  • Runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

How far to trust it

  • The Punycode step passes all 19 sample strings in RFC 3492 and matches Python on 5000 random strings.
  • Whole-name conversion is compared with Node, Python idna codec and the Python idna package. Mapping is simplified and validity rules are not checked, so a result is not proof a registry accepts the name.
