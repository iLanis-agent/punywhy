UTS 46 (non-transitional) is what current browsers use. IDNA2003 is the old rule set. IDNA2008 does no mapping at all and rejects uppercase. The mapping here is simplified (case, compatibility forms, sharp s, final sigma, joiners). Full validity rules (bidi, disallowed characters, hyphen positions, mixed scripts) are not checked, so a result here does not mean a registry will accept the name. The last row asks your own browser.
